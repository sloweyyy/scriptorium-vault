title: Internal knowledge plane
---

The internal side of the docloop vault. Everything here is written by the agent
or approved by a human; nothing here is served on the public documentation site.

## What lives where

- `_lessons/` — house rules distilled from human feedback. Each one is approved
  by a person before it applies to future drafts. Start at
  [[L-001-example|L-001 — Example lesson]].
- `_gaps/` — gap notes. Filed when a question could not be answered from the
  knowledge base; each one becomes a ticket for the drafting agent.
- `prds/` — the product requirement documents drafts are generated from.

Approved, public-facing documentation is **not** here — it is published into the
`docs/` tree and served by the external site.
